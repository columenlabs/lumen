# Edit widget

Every widget on an editable page has a **⋯** menu with **Edit widget**. It opens one dialog for the
whole widget: its type (Table, Metric, Allocation or Chart, each with its styles), name, width and
link colour on the left with its fields, settings, rows, formulas and controls, and on the right
the data it reads, field by field. **Save** writes the change into the view config through
`onConfigChange`.

## What the app does

- Nothing beyond `onConfigChange`: the dialog, its pickers and checks are the kit's.
- The menu also offers Duplicate, Move up, Move down and Remove.
